# LeetCode-style coding tests in Vietnamese software hiring

- **Question:** How often do companies in Vietnam use LeetCode-style coding tests when hiring for CS and software jobs?
- **Finding:**
  - *No measured rate found.* No survey, job-board report (ITviec, TopCV, TopDev, VietnamWorks) or study gives the share of Vietnamese employers that use algorithm or LeetCode-style tests. Any percentage quoted for "Vietnam" would be invented.
  - What exists is candidate self-reports. Glassdoor reports for FPT Software, VNG and Grab's Vietnam roles describe an online or paper test on data structures and algorithms, sometimes "LeetCode medium", before the technical interview. A VNG fresher report (Sep 2023) lists: CV scan, entry test on data structures and algorithms, then technical interviews on CS fundamentals. FPT Software reports describe an online technical assessment on data structures and algorithms. Grab is described in prep blogs as an online assessment with two LeetCode-medium problems.
  - Pattern, *checker's inference from a handful of reports*: large outsourcing firms, product companies (VNG) and multinationals (Grab, Uber and similar) commonly include an algorithm round for software engineer and fresher roles; smaller firms are reported to prefer a take-home task or a technical conversation. Neither claim is measured.
  - One opinion piece (e27, Jul 2026) says most Vietnamese interview processes "test syntax, algorithms, and framework knowledge", and argues this misses what companies need. It gives no counts for coding tests.
  - A widely shared "73% of hiring managers prioritise LeetCode" figure (dev.to post, 500 managers, 42 countries) is not Vietnam-specific and the post appears to rest on synthetic data; do not cite it.
- **Evidence strength:** *anecdotal.* Sample: a handful of anonymous Glassdoor and prep-blog reports (roughly 5 to 10 read in search snippets, not counted systematically, some from 2022 to 2023). No survey with a stated sample size covers coding tests in Vietnam.

- **Limits:** Self-reports over-represent memorable or large-company processes and are not a random sample. Search snippets, not full pages, were read for Glassdoor and Jointaro. ITviec and TopCV interview-format data and Viettel were not found. Role level (fresher vs senior) and company size are not separated. Treat it as "common at large and foreign-owned firms, rate unknown", not as a share.
