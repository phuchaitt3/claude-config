# Undergraduate thesis as research experience

**Question:** Can an undergraduate thesis be listed as "Research Experience" on a resume?

**Finding:**

- **Yes, it counts as research experience.** Hanover College names "Senior thesis" first in its list for a Research Experience section. Binghamton lists honors theses and capstone projects among the things that give students research experience, and allows the title "undergraduate or graduate researcher" even for class projects.
- **Placement varies, and that is a choice rather than a rule.** The common default is a line under the degree in Education. Pitzer says to consider moving the thesis to the Experience section if it is "especially relevant to the role" or involved "significant independent work", with bullets on skills, tools and outcomes. Pitzer also offers "Projects or Research Experience" as a category name. Binghamton suggests a "Research Experience" heading when there are several entries, and placing a single one inside another suitable section.
- **Unpaid research should not sit under a bare "Experience" heading**, according to one guide: Carnegie Mellon's Washington Semester guide says "Experience" as a standalone title "implies employment" and suggests renaming such a section, for example to "Research Experience".
- **For industry roles,** Binghamton advises stressing transferable skills and outcomes over technical detail.
- Checker's inference: a thesis with substantial independent work, aimed at a role that asks for research, meets Pitzer's test for leaving Education. It should still be labelled as a thesis, so that it is not read as a research post.

- [Pitzer College, resume content](https://www.pitzer.edu/offices/career-services/resumecv-writing-guide/writing-effective-resume-content) (university career center; read in full).
- [Binghamton, adding research experience](https://careertools.binghamton.edu/resources/how-to-add-research-experience-to-your-resume-or-cv/) (university career center; read in full).
- [Carnegie Mellon, Washington Semester resume guide](https://www.cmu.edu/cmist/washington-center/washington-semester-program/wsp-resume-guide-new-7.20.23.pdf) (university program guide, 2023; text extracted from the PDF).
- [Princeton, writing about research experience](https://pcur.princeton.edu/2022/10/tips-for-writing-about-your-research-experience-even-if-you-dont-think-you-have-any) (student-written university blog; read in full; does not name theses, but treats coursework papers as research).

**Limits:** All sources are US university guidance written for students; none reports what employers actually do, and none covers Vietnam. The Carnegie Mellon line comes from a single program's guide, written for a policy-focused semester program rather than technical roles. Whether a thesis is real research depends on its content; these sources cover labels, not quality. Guidance on theses for research-engineering roles specifically was not found.
