# Resume dates for a project revisited after a gap

- **Question:** When a project from years ago gets new work now, should its date range run "start -- Present", or show the separate periods, so the reader does not assume continuous development?
- **Finding:**
  - **No guide addresses projects revisited later directly.** General date guidance: dates must be accurate and consistent; "Present" is for something ongoing; an ATS expects month-year digits and the word "Present".
  - **The closest documented case is returning to an employer ("boomerang").** Guides list each stint with its own dates, either as separate entries or under one heading with an overall span and per-stint dates underneath. They do not merge stints into one continuous range.
  - *Checker's inference:* "Sep. 2024 -- Present" for work done in Sep. 2024 – Jan. 2025 and again from Oct. 2026 implies two years of continuous work, which a reader may take as fact and ask about. Showing both periods ("Sep. 2024 -- Jan. 2025, Oct. 2026 -- Present") is accurate and follows the stint practice; the cost is a longer date field. A single "Present" range is defensible only with a visible cue that the work came in phases.
