# Is a resume ready to submit

- **Question:** What are the refinement steps before submitting a tailored resume, and how do you know it is ready?
- **Finding:** University career-centre checklists agree on a short final pass:
  1. **Tailored to the posting.** Keywords from the posting appear in the summary and the bullets (Villanova, UNC); tailoring is the last step before submitting, and you keep a PDF copy of the posting, since postings get taken down (Duke). See `ats-keyword-matching` for which keywords count.
  2. **Length and layout.** One page for most undergraduates; a simple, clean layout an ATS can read (UNC and others).
  3. **Text extraction test.** Select all text in the PDF and paste it into a plain-text editor: name, phone and email must come through, sections in order, nothing missing or scrambled. If the text cannot be selected, the PDF is an image. Two-column layouts, tables, text boxes and icon fonts are the usual failures. This is a first pass, not a guarantee for every ATS (several vendor guides; no vendor documentation).
  4. **Proofread, then a second reader.** Spelling, grammar and formatting (Wesleyan); read a printed copy, even backwards, then have a friend or colleague read it (UNC and others). Spell out acronyms the reader may not know.
  5. **Links work.** Email, LinkedIn, GitHub and work samples open the right page (UNC).
  6. **File.** PDF unless the employer asks for another format; a professional file name such as `Lastname_Firstname_Resume.pdf` (UNC, Montana State).
  - *Checker's inference:* "ready" means every item above passes and every claim on the page can be defended in an interview. Refinement past that has diminishing returns against the deadline; the first screen is a short skim (see `resume-bullet-clarity`).
