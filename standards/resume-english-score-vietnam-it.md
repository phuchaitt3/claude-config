# English test scores on IT resumes in Vietnam

- **Question:** How much does an English test score (IELTS) matter in Vietnamese software hiring, and where does it go on a resume?
- **Finding:**
  - English is the soft skill Vietnamese IT employers ask for most. In ITviec's 2024–2025 survey, 40.3% of companies named English proficiency, the top soft skill for the first half of 2025 (as reported by VIR). ITviec's posting data shows demand for English growing fastest of all tracked skills: +52% year on year and +87% quarter on quarter in Q2 2025, and English-related postings up 52.9% year on year in Q3 2025.
  - English is scarce. TopDev's 2023 report estimates that about 5% of the Vietnamese workforce is proficient, and says English is moving from nice-to-have to must-have for developers. A VnExpress source says the top AI salaries go to engineers with experience and English.
  - On listing a score, Vietnamese career sites (VietNamWorks, CareerLink) advise including IELTS for international companies and English-heavy roles when the score is 6.5 or higher, or meets the posting's bar. Below about 5.5, describe the skill instead of giving the number. Write the test's full name and score. Joboko notes that employers often treat a score older than about 2 years as expired, which matches IELTS's own 2-year validity.
  - Placement: no source compares a separate "Languages" section with a row in the skills section. *Checker's inference:* both are common. The score is what is read, so on a one-page resume a skills-row entry costs no signal, while on a resume with space a separate line makes it easier to spot for international employers who screen for English.
